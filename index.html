Projects

Maziwa Plus: dairy yield and feed tracking 2026, in progress

An app for small dairy farms, built from my parents' farm outwards. Farmers log milk per milking and the feed that went in, using the measures they actually use on the farm (buckets, tins, a truckload of napier), and the app turns that into cost per litre and margin over time. Vets and the milk buyer have their own separate views for insemination, vaccination dates and notices. Feed suggestions stay off until a farm has enough recorded days to support them, and never recommend more than a small change at a time.

Streamlit, PostgreSQL (Supabase, plain SQL). Team entry for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon.

Berth scheduling: greedy heuristic vs. exact MILP 2026

The same port scheduling problem solved two ways and compared on the same instances. The exact model (PuLP/CBC) beats the heuristic by about 38% on small cases; on larger, congested ones the solver can't match the heuristic in the time allowed. Includes a regression test for a floating-point bug I found along the way.
